Why menus and cone families behave differently

For each fixed gamble, eventually Dₙ agrees with L. Any finite list that misses L must therefore miss some Dₙ. The fixed menu above does that for every n from 8 onward.

There is still a different conflict at each n. The infinite menu of all negated conflict gambles hits every Dₙ and misses L. It would be available in the infinitary version of the problem. The paper proves the general statements.
