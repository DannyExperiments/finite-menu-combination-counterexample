# Claim scope

- The counterexample concerns finite menus of real gambles and arbitrary original nonempty representing families. It uses a common two-outcome label.
- The failure is specifically a failure of pointwise cone combination followed by the representation map. The witnessing menu is a nonempty singleton.
- The source’s Proposition 11 allows infinite menus. Its positive result is compatible with the counterexample.
- A compactness application of known representation theory gives equality after taking membership closures of both families. Finite families are a special case.
- No general statement about all canonical representation algebras or marginalization is claimed. The partial Lean check covers the arithmetic identities and rational witnesses. [Literature audit](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md)
