# Mathematical review

All probability weights are positive, so the strict-expectation and lexicographic cones are coherent. For a fixed gamble the first nonzero lexicographic component determines its eventual sign. That agreement is simultaneous over a finite menu. At every index the displayed midpoint gives a different opposite-gamble conflict, so each pair's natural extension is inconsistent. The constant negative singleton separates the menu results.

The compact-family condition works because finite-menu hitting conditions are clopen and coherent cones are a closed subspace of a product of two-point spaces. Compactness gives a finite separating menu for any cone containing no entire family member. Applying the prior conjunctive representation establishes the scoped corollary.
